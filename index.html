Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

4.5 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"ARIRANG doesn't sound like seven men proving they still fit together — it sounds like they stopped worrying about it."

ARIRANG (2026): the reunion album that isn't about reuniting. The seven of them wrote and lived through this one together in LA, and it shows in how unbothered it sounds — fourteen tracks that move through easy pop, ballad, and genre experiments without once stopping to explain the gap. Diplo, Ryan Tedder, and El Guincho all show up in the credits, but RM's hand across the lyric sheet is what keeps fourteen very different tracks reading as one thought.

Title track "SWIM" sets the tone early — a mid-tempo alt-pop cut about moving at your own pace instead of racing the current, which reads less like a comeback single and more like a mission statement for the album around it.
